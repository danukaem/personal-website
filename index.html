About

Who I am

I'm a senior software engineer with 7+ years of experience in web and desktop application development, including two years on-site in Singapore. I hold a BSc (Hons) in Engineering from the University of Moratuwa and a Master's in Enterprise Application Development from SLIIT.

Day to day I design and build Spring Boot microservices and REST APIs, paired with React or Angular front ends, backed by PostgreSQL, MySQL or MongoDB and deployed on AWS (Lambda, ECS, EC2). I've worked on Kafka-based real-time pipelines, containerized deployments with Docker and Kubernetes, and secure authentication systems built on Keycloak and JWT — including two years delivering banking platform services for DBS Bank Singapore.

Before software I worked as a materials and quality engineer across steel, cable and construction projects. That background still shows up in how I work: measure first, test properly, and don't ship what you haven't verified.
